A while ago the Italian magazine **Voglio Vivere Così** — the one that tells the
stories of people who decide to change their life and leave — interviewed me.
The headline they chose says it better than I could: *“In Brazil, there’s a love
for life.”*

That is exactly what I found here. I left the province of Milan, arrived in
Brazil in 2017 almost by chance — a volunteering project — and never went back.
Today I live in Vidigal, the favela over the sea between Leblon and the Dois
Irmãos peaks, and I have been guiding here in Rio de Janeiro for nine years.

## Why I’m telling you this

Because it is the heart of Vidapiena. “A full life” — *vita piena* — is not a
slogan: it is why I take people *inside* the communities, not past them. In the
interview I told a simple story: in Italy my next-door neighbour never said
hello; here the neighbourhood throws a party once a month for everyone’s
birthdays. This is the Rio I want to show you — not from behind a car window, but
on foot, greeting the people we meet.

> The difference between seeing Rio and living it is right here: you come in as a
> guest, with respect, where life actually happens.
